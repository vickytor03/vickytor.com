Granny Model

Here is my Granny model, based on a sketch by Ruslan Suleimanov.

I used Zbrush and Maya for modeling, Substance Painter for texturing, Houdini for grooming, and Redshift for shading, lighting and rendering.
The character was rigged and posed using an autorig I coded myself in Python.See more.
