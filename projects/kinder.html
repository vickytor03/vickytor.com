Kinder

Client: Kinder

VFX Studio: House of Parliament

Role: Previz, Animation, Procedural FX, Look Dev, Lighting

Tools: Maya, Houdini, Arnold

Contribution & Impact:

My primary contribution to this Kinder campaign was in the Racing Royale spot, in which I worked on the previz, animation, look dev, and lighting. I also developed a procedural system for animating the race tracks' appearance, enabling efficient iteration while maintaining precise artistic control.

In addition, I supported the Unicorn Safari team in the development of the animated cloud characters, refining the assets and developing an alternative rendering workflow to achieve the desired final look under production constraints.
