Wegovy

Client: Wegovy

VFX Studio: House of Parliament

Role: Modeling, Texturing, Look Dev, Animation, Lighting

Tools: Maya, ZBrush, Substance Painter, Arnold, Houdini

Contribution & Impact:

I was responsible for the CG arm extensions for Danielle Brooks, handling the work from modeling through texturing, look dev, animation, and lighting.

The task required closely matching the actress' real hands and jean jacket, incorporating reference and elements captured on set to ensure continuity with the live-action plate. I worked closely with the compositing team, providing the necessary passes and technical information to support a seamless integration between the CG renders and the plate.
