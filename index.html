About

I am Victor D'Anzi (VICKYTOR), a 21-year-old Italian-Belgian artist based in New York. I am a 3D Animator and film maker.

My passion has always been storytelling: from filling out notebooks with drawings of original stories, to my love for cinema and acting.

I decided to enter the world of animation after High School, to express my creativity. After studying CG Animation at the European Institute of Design (IED), in Rome, I am now enrolled in School of Visual Arts (SVA)'s MFA in Computer Arts.
