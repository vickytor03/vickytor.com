About

I am Victor D’Anzi, an Italian-Belgian CG Generalist and film maker based in New York. Raised in the eternal city of Rome, I was surrounded by art and film from an early age, studying theater and cinema, and acting professionally as a teenager. In high school, I discovered animation as a career path that united my passions and gave me a way to fully express my creativity.

What excites me most about animation is curating every detail, and pushing the technical tools at my disposal to enhance storytelling and character development.

I studied CG Animation at the European Institute of Design (IED) in Rome, where I was Character Animator and Pipeline Technical Director on Artifex, a short film commissioned by View Conference 2023. I am currently pursuing an MFA in Computer Arts at the School of Visual Arts (SVA) in New York.
