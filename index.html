About

I am Victor D’Anzi, an Italian-Belgian CG Generalist and film maker based in New York. Raised in the eternal city of Rome, I was surrounded by art and film from an early age, studying theater and cinema, and acting professionally as a teenager. In high school, I discovered animation and vfx as a career path that united my passions and gave me a way to fully express my creativity.

What excites me most about computer graphics is the ability to shape every detail, pushing the technical tools at my disposal to enhance storytelling and character development.

I hold an MFA in Computer Arts from School of Visual Arts (SVA), New York. After directing my first animated short film, Ellis' Reflection, during my graduate studies, I began working in New York City's VFX industry, contributing to commercial campaigns ranging from broadcast spots to Super Bowl advertising.
